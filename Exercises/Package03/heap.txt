43 is already greater than its child, so no swap is needed.

2.  MAXHEAPIFY(A, 0) A = [18, 80, 34, 66, 49, 55, 90, 27]

At index 0, the largest of 18, 80, and 34 is 80. Swap 18 and 80.

The recursive MAXHEAPIFY call on index 1 then moves 66 above 18. That
embedded call is treated as one change.

Step Array 0 [18, 80, 34, 66, 49, 55, 90, 27] 1 [80, 18, 34, 66, 49, 55,
90, 27] 2 [80, 66, 34, 18, 49, 55, 90, 27]
